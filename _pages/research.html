Research

I study how robots can learn human assembly skills, from planning precise motions to adapting to contact forces.

Motion Planning through Human Demonstration

How can a robot learn to make the precise movements needed for assembly? This project uses human demonstrations to plan robot motions for tasks such as L-shaped insertion, tenon insertion, and peg rotation.

Advanced Robotics · 2020

Robotic Imitation of Human Assembly Skills

A robot assembling parts needs to adapt when contact forces change. This project uses hierarchical imitation learning to learn a nominal trajectory and reinforcement learning to adapt the forces applied during assembly. The framework was tested in simulation and on real hardware.
